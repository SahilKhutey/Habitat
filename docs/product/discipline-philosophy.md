# The Habitat Discipline & Behavioral Philosophy

## Core Axiom: Voluntary Commitment Over Humiliation
Habitat is designed from first principles as an instrument of self-mastery. The product rejects cheap gamification, humiliating punishment loops, and coercive lockouts.

$$\textbf{Clarity} + \textbf{Action} + \textbf{Irrefutable Proof} \implies \textbf{Permanent Behavioral Sovereignity}$$

### 1. Alarm $\neq$ Notification
A traditional alarm is an ephemeral beep that the user can dismiss while half-asleep in a state of cognitive inertia. In Habitat:
* An alarm is not a dismissal target; it is the **commencement of a Mission**.
* The siren will escalate volume ($70\text{dB} \to 85\text{dB} \to 100\text{dB}$) every 5 minutes until physical evidence is captured.

### 2. Task vs Mission
* **Task**: The immutable archetype of discipline (e.g., "10 Strict Push-ups", "Drink 500ml Water", "Make Your Bed").
* **Mission**: A single temporal instantiation of a task with an exact timestamp, resistance duration ($\Delta t_R$), and proof record.

### 3. Proof Integrity & Camera Guardrails
* Unrestricted gallery uploads are strictly disabled by default.
* The device must capture live photos or minimum 10-second videos with sensor verification to prevent cheating.

### 4. Grace Vault & Psychological Anti-Fragility
* Missing a day should not destroy a user's morale or reset months of progress to zero.
* The **Grace Vault** holds up to 3 defensive shields earned through consistent 14-day streak milestones, automatically protecting unbroken streaks when life emergencies happen.
