# Habitat MVP — User Testing Guide

## Step-by-Step Offline Validation Protocol

1. **Install Habitat MVP APK** onto your Android device or run on iOS TestFlight.
2. **Turn OFF Wi-Fi and Mobile Data** (or enable Airplane Mode).
3. **Open Habitat:** Enter a local display name (no passwords or cloud accounts).
4. **Select a Task:** Choose from templates (e.g. *Brush Teeth*, *15 Pushups*, *Make Bed*) or create a custom task.
5. **Set an Alarm:** Schedule 2 minutes in the future.
6. **Lock the Device:** Close the app and wait.
7. **Alarm Verification:**
   - Confirm the alarm rings while locked.
   - Ignore the alarm once to verify the **5-minute repeat escalation**.
8. **Perform Task & Capture Proof:** Open the task, take a photo or record video proof.
9. **Verify Completion:** Confirm the alarm stops permanently, XP is awarded, and streak increments to 1.
10. **Submit Feedback:** Go to Settings $\to$ Send Feedback $\to$ Export Feedback to share your review.
