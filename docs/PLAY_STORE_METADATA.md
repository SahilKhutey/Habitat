## Full Description

**Habitat is the anti-cheat personal discipline platform that transforms intentions into unbreakable daily habits.**

Most alarm apps are easy to dismiss, snooze, or ignore. Habitat introduces **Mission-Driven Proof**: the only way to disarm your high-priority wake-up alarm is by completing your scheduled physical or environment mission and providing real photographic or computer vision proof.

### 🔥 Key Features:
- **Unignorable Multi-Stage Alarm**: High-priority full-screen sirens that override Doze mode and escalate volume from 70% to 100% until your mission is completed.
- **Real-Time Computer Vision Proof**: Uses MoveNet on-device pose estimation to verify exercise missions (e.g. push-ups, stretches) with anti-spoof liveness checks.
- **Durable Offline-First Architecture**: 100% functional without an internet connection. Your schedules, alarms, and streak histories never depend on cloud availability.
- **Grace Token Vault & Streak Protection**: Build compounding momentum. Earn Grace Tokens to protect your streak during genuine emergencies without cheating your progress.
- **Tactical AI Discipline Coach**: Daily morning briefings, recovery score analysis, and behavioral insights designed to eliminate cognitive friction.

### 🔒 Privacy & Data Safety:
- **Zero Raw Video Harvesting**: Camera streams are analyzed locally or in transient encrypted sessions. No surveillance, no selling data.
- **Zero Third-Party Trackers**: Pure performance and accountability.
