# Habitat Discipline Platform — v1.0.0 Release Notes

## Overview
Habitat v1.0.0 is the complete, production-hardened personal discipline platform across Android, iPhone (iOS), Web, and Backend services.

## Core Capabilities
- **Multi-Channel Alarms:** Exact wake alarms (`AlarmManager`, full-screen intent, and `UNUserNotificationCenter` local notifications).
- **Computer Vision Verification:** Instant proof verification for morning pushups, teeth brushing, and outdoor sunlight.
- **5-Minute Non-Lethal Escalation:** Deterministic interval repeat until proof is submitted and validated.
- **Immutable XP Ledger:** Idempotent rewards ($50 \cdot L(L-1)$ level curve) with zero duplicate awards.
- **Intelligent Planning & Personalization:** Privacy-safe behavioral pattern analysis with user-controlled non-coercive adaptation.
- **Bounded Health Domain:** Decoupled exercise, water/hydration, and sleep tracking with GDPR-compliant right-to-be-forgotten deletion.
- **Personal Discipline Coach:** AI conversational guidance bounded by an action allowlist and strict safety filter.
- **Social & Accountability Platform:** Encrypted privacy controls, shared challenges, and partner progress nudges.
